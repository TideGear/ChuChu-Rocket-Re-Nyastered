# Presentation audit

This inventory compares the current playable routes with the iPad HD 1.0 original. The Dreamcast presentation, alternate modes, and every sound and animation trigger still need their own full reference pass. Function names below refer to the local original binaries and their decompiles.

| Trigger or screen | What the original does | Port coverage and open checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold start | The iPad screen table enters Sega, then the BH logo, then Sonic Team, then Title. The startup screens have their own process, render, and transition callbacks. Sonic Team requests song 14 when it reaches its reveal state. | The executable starts at Title when title assets are available. All three startup screens and their timing, audio, and skip behavior are open. Audit the Dreamcast startup separately. |
| Title and menu | Animated title art and prompt, fades, a moving menu mascot, a description bubble, selection/confirmation/refusal cues, and a tutorial-completion gate. | Main iPad title and menu paths are present. Device variants, first-run progression and the full menu transition and audio inventory remain open. |
| Navigation and overlays | Stage select, battle setup, Options, Help, Credits and Pause each have screen-specific highlights, button feedback, borders, fades, audio and input gating. | The port covers parts of these screens. Check each cold entry, back transition, pause/resume/quit, missing-asset path, and locale. Tutorial, Challenge, costume selection, Puzzle Edit and multiplayer setup coverage are especially incomplete. |
| Arrow placement and expiry | Placement and refusal have branch-specific cues. Battle arrows can shrink after a hit and blink before expiry. The original also has separate arrow ghost and cursor trail callbacks. | Main placement, hit and expiry visuals are present. Verify refusal semantics and cooldowns, drag previews, ghosts, player tags, and trails. |
| Mouse or cat walking and turning | Angle smoothing, direction sheets, walk phases, and per-entity height and offsets. | Walking and turning are present. Audit height, spawning, animation during events, and crossings against the original. Dreamcast character models have only been exported and rendered offline. |
| Cat catches a mouse | iPad `SmpColAnalyze` flags contact, `MO_MouseExe` changes the update function, and `MO_MouseExe_Eaten` can create `MO_Obake` for standard characters when fewer than 12 ghosts are active. The ghost moves and cycles through eight frames, and its task deletes at execution 48. A Unicorn check verified ghost registration at counts 0 and 11, refusal at 12, and no direct SFX call in those cases. | Shared-screen Battle now draws the ghost using image slots 44–46. Its draw calls match the iPad overhead animation. Puzzle integration, alternate views and costumes, and audio checks in other versions remain open. |
| Mouse or cat falls into a hole | iPad switches to dedicated fall update and draw callbacks. This is a separate trigger from being caught. | Fall presentation and its full audio path remain open. Check puzzle failure, battle respawn, and hole/wall occlusion. |
| Mouse or cat enters a rocket | Original entry uses character-specific state and draw callbacks, scoring, a rocket response, and sound routing that distinguishes ordinary mice, golden mice and cats. | Battle scores and some sound hooks exist. The current mouse score hook uses slot 6 for every mouse, while HD 1.0 explicitly requests slot 8 for gold on its verified score branch. Verify the complete audio path and entry animation. |
| Countdown and roulette | Round/start and time warnings have separate tasks. Roulette shows the outcome on the drum, with spin, stop, voice and event-specific effects. | Drum and voice timing verified. Music fades, volume changes, countdown and other per-event audio/animation branches still need coverage. Cat Attack's animated cats are missing. |
| Puzzle clear, loss, battle result and match end | Dedicated result, focus, rocket, smoke, flash and congratulations routines. The original field rocket lid replaces the standing rocket and can redraw a foreground wall. | iPad lid, lift-off and smoke match in the headless task comparison. Live field launch triggers, foreground wall redraw, glow, and complete results and match-end presentation remain open. |

See [audio.md](audio.md) for source and cue mapping, [animation.md](animation.md) for character animation, [dreamcast_models.md](dreamcast_models.md) for the offline models, and [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) for verification results.
