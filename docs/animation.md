# Character animation notes (living document, 2026-11-13)

## Mobile sprite sheets (`ret_all/r0447` = cat, `r0503` = mouse, `r0484` = gold mouse; frames in `.frames.bin`: count + 9 floats each)
- The cat sheet has **32 frames that form a pre-rendered turntable**: the same cat rotating through 32 angles (frame 0 facing the camera, 8 frames per quarter turn; verified by contact sheet `local/shots/cat_frames.png`). They are rotation steps, **not a walk cycle**. The app currently shows only frames heading*8 (4 headings), so characters look static while moving.
- The Android code (`FUN_00028ffc`, same code base as iOS) advances the walk phase by 0.45 per tick (wrap at 20.0) and smooths the turn angle with 16-bit `diffAngle`/`adjustAngle`; the turntable index follows that smoothed angle, so turns animate through the intermediate frames.
- Next: read the exact angle smoothing and the walk-phase use (the bobbing/leg motion may be a separate sheet or a transform), implement turning through the 32 frames driven by the original angle logic, then the other states (rocket entry, hole fall, eaten, spawn) from their sheets; Dreamcast 3D keyframes/morph animation is covered in dreamcast_models.md.

## Turn animation (implemented 2026-11-13)
From the Android decompile: each entity has a current angle (+0x20), a target angle (+0x24 = heading << 14 on a 16-bit circle), and a per-tick step (+0x28 = `int(speed * 32768 / 12)`; the divisor 12.0 is the literal read at 0x35264 for mice and 0x29284 for cats; mouse 1.5 -> 4096 = 0x1000, i.e. 4 ticks per quarter turn). Each tick: `diffAngle(cur, target)` (absolute signed 16-bit difference); if it is below 0x7f01, `adjustAngle` moves the angle numerically toward the target by at most the step (not wrapped, so e.g. East->South turns the long way round, as in the original) and snaps when within one step; otherwise (about-face) the angle just advances by the step. The app (`App::stepAngles`) runs this once per sim tick and draws turntable frame `angle >> 11`; verified headless (mice drawn at intermediate angles in mid-battle frames). Not yet done: walk bobbing, rocket entry/hole fall/eaten/spawn animations, exact frame timing of the other states, the Dreamcast 3D versions.

## Correction (2026-11-13): turn direction
The first version of the turn animation used the numeric sign of (target - current) to pick the direction, so a heading change such as South -> East turned 270 degrees the long way round, and the note above wrongly called that an original quirk. The original `Core::adjustAngle` decides by the sign of the **16-bit wrapped** difference (`iVar2 * 0x10000 < 0`), i.e. the shortest way. `game/core/angle.hpp` now holds `subAngle`/`diffAngle`/`adjustAngle`/`stepAngle` exactly as decompiled; `game/tests/angle_test.cpp` compares them with results recorded from the original code (`tools/oracle/make_angle_golden.py`, 4,000 random cases: 0 differences). Measured in 6 full battles with `turn_stats`: 7.7% of character-ticks are mid-turn, only 0.25% face more than 90 degrees away from their heading, all of them around the 206 genuine 180-degree reversals (a U-turn: the character keeps moving while it spins round, as the original logic does).


## Walk cycles (implemented 2026-11-13; corrects the first section)
The first section called the 32-frame sheets the only character art; they are not what the game draws while characters walk. From `CA_CatDrawSTPriority` (Android 0x29810) and the mouse draw `FUN_0003582c` (0x3582c), with the literal pool read from the .so (the Ghidra image base is 0x10000 above the file offset): the draw picks one of three textures by the current angle `a` (16-bit, field +0x20): **back view** if `a` is in [0x6001, 0xA000], **front view** if `a` is outside [0x2001, 0xE000], **side view** otherwise (cat ids 0x33 side, 0x34 back, 0x35 front; mouse table index type*3 + view with view 0 side, 1 back, 2 front), and the frame is the walk phase. Cat: phase += 0.45 per moving tick, wrapping at 20.0 (`FUN_00028ffc`), frame = (int)phase of a 20-frame sheet (iPad pack r0420 side, r0429 back, r0438 front, verified by contact sheet: a walking cat). Mouse: frame = TI_GetGlobalTime mod 11 (`FUN_000353e0` writes +0x34), 11-frame sheets (normal r0491/r0495/r0499, gold r0472/r0476/r0480). Each frame record's xoff/yoff place the sprite relative to the entity's position (iPad pack: 64 px per cell). The 32-frame sheets (r0447, r0503, ...) are therefore not used for walking; what they are for (title/roulette/pose uses) is not yet traced. Unverified: how the original mirrors the side view for west-facing movement (the flag is not visible in the decompile; the port mirrors when the angle is in (0x2000, 0x8000)), and which mouse table row the roulette-type (type 2) mouse uses (the port draws it as a normal mouse). The mouse also has a hop (`+0x30` velocity, `+0x18` height) that is not drawn yet.

## Rockets (first reading for the playtest report, 2026-11-13; not implemented)
- The rocket sheets are the six 49-frame textures `r0651, r0663, r0675, r0687, r0699, r0711` (one per colour) of the iPad character pack: frames 0..6 are the sphere with its equator splitting open (closed -> open), more frames repeat the split from other angles, and the last frames (42..48) are the dome lid opening on a black interior (viewed on `r0651`).
- Battle drawing (`FUN_0003be00`, called from `RO_Rocket4DrawPriority0..3` through `FUN_0003c0c8`) passes an openness of -1.0, which selects **frame 0 (closed)**, so in play the rocket is a closed sphere whose size grows: `RO_RocketSetPukuPuku(rocket)` adds a constant (literal at 0x3b05c, value not read yet) to a per-rocket "puku puku" value, capped at 1.5, each time a mouse enters; the draw scale is derived from it (times 0.8). Where the value decays again is not read yet.
- The open-lid frames are used by `RO_FutaOpenDrawPriority` (task value +0x30 times 6, capped at frame 6) in the **departure sequence at the end of a battle**: `FUN_0003bb18` (phase 0 of the four-battle loop's result handling) creates, for each rocket that has mice, three tasks (lid open `RO_FutaOpen`, moving rocket `RO_GoGoRocket`, smoke `RocketSmoke`), plays `ChuSECall(0x15)` and vibrates; the winner rocket(s) lift off.
- So the missing "rocket animation" is two things: the grow-on-entry pulse during play and the lid-open / lift-off sequence at the end. Both need the constants of the tasks above (growth step, decay, lid speed, lift-off path) before they can be drawn at the original timing.

## Rocket size pulse (implemented 2026-11-14)
`RO_RocketSetPukuPuku(player)` (Android 0x3b030): adds 0.2 (literal 0x3e4ccccd) to the rocket's size value on every mouse that enters it, capped at 1.5 (0x3fc00000); it is called from the mouse task together with `SC_AddScore`. The draw function `FUN_0003be00` multiplies the value by 0.8 (0x3f4ccccd) for the closed sphere. No decay was found in the functions that reference the table (`RO_ShuffleRockets`, the draw functions, `FUN_0003bb18`), so the growth is cumulative within a round. The port grows `BattleGame::puku[player]` the same way and draws the sphere at CELL x value. Start value 1.0 is read from the puzzle manager (`RO_StartRocketPzManager` 0x3b798); the battle manager's initialisation and the on-screen size at value 1.0 (the 0.8 factor against the sprite) were not verified. Still missing: the lid-open and lift-off sequence at the end of a battle (`FUN_0003bb18`, `RO_FutaOpenDrawPriority`).
The iPad HD 1.0 cat hole task (`CA_CatExe_Fall`, 0x147b4) turns the cat by 0x1000 per update as its scale falls from 1 to 0. `CA_CatDrawFall2D` (0x148e4) queues callback 0x14969 in group 15 at truncated world Z with minimum depth -64. The callback draws image 43, frame `(angle & 0xffff) >> 11`, at the field position with no mirroring and full opacity. Image 43 maps to iPad texture record 364 and frame record 376 and is now in the default extractor.

`tools/oracle/hd_cat_fall.py` verified the shrinking, rotation, draw arguments, and retirement gates at three positions across ten mode and stage combinations. The measured task lifetime is 30 updates in Battle, Team Battle and Challenge stage remainders 3–4, and 151 updates in Puzzle-class modes and Challenge remainders 0–2. Mode 5 runs for 30 updates and deletes the task. The probe returns zero generators to distinguish the branch that tries to respawn from the branch that deletes immediately. Five additional fixtures used the original movement, field collision, and generator routines with a seeded field, four generators, and controlled RNG returns. On collision the cat keeps its position and angle, sets the hole flag, and switches to the fall tasks without requesting a sound. On update 30 it draws one random value, selects the generator with truncation of count × 0.99 × RNG, snaps to its center, and restores the walking tasks. The current angle is the generator heading plus 0x1000 while the target remains at the generator heading. Confirm live overlap ordering before drawing the effect in the port.
