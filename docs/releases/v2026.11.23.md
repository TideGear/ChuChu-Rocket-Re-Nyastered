# ChuChu Rocket! Re-Nyastered 2026-11-23

Dreamcast sound arrives. Every Dreamcast sound effect and the music now come from your own disc image, with no BIOS and no mobile files needed.

## New

- **Dreamcast sound effects.** Setup renders all 128 sound-effect cues from the disc's own sound driver and sound bank (`MANATEE.DRV`, `CHU2_SE.MLT`) with a built-in pure-Python replay of the console's sound chip, and the game plays them. Compared with the real console running in Flycast, every cue's voices match sample for sample; 120 of 128 cues also produce exactly the same notes (8 cues differ in a few notes). Setup takes about three minutes in total.
- **Dreamcast music rules.** CD tracks repeat the way the original does (the whole track from its first sound, not a guessed loop point); the end-screen, jingle and two other songs play once. The Sonic Team start-up jingle comes from the disc (CD track 18), so it no longer needs the mobile files. The Options > Music test row now plays the selected song.
- **Cue 7 (rocket) throttle** as in the original: repeated calls within three frames play once.
- **Puzzle rendering:** the shared depth scene now covers every Dreamcast Puzzle frame (9 launch frames match the console better, none worse).

## Updating

Run Easy Setup again in RecompLauncher (or `ccr --prepare`): the game will remind you. Setup adds the sound effects and refreshes the art.

## Known unfinished work

- Sound: the console's start/stop latency (under 3 ms), the five-slot cut-off (a sixth overlapping effect cuts the oldest) and the per-channel vibrato phase are not modeled; 8 cues differ in a few notes; voice lines (`VOICE.AFS`), music fades, and most tracks' loops are not yet compared with the console.
- Not yet built or verified: The Basics, Costumes, the roulette wheel, Puzzle Edit, GBA Character Edit, online and wireless play, Jump Pack rumble, Keyboard F-key page; the Battle round-start caption and star twinkles are not drawn; Options General and 4P settings do not change game rules yet. Details in `docs/audit.md`.
